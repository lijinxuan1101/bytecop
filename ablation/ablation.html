From the full stack to Spatial only

We started with the full stack: a CLIP spatial tower, an RGPA forensic tower, gated fusion of their logits, and MBE at eval. Then we removed each piece on the same 5,000-image WildFake val slice. Keep a piece only if it raises official score (0.5 × AUC_clean + 0.5 × mean of 14 degradations). MBE, RGPA, and fusion all lose. We ship Spatial only — OpenCLIP ViT-H/14, taken at minimum val_loss, not at the later AUC peak.

Why start with two towers and enhancement Sharing wipes high-frequency traces first. The spatial tower reads semantics; RGPA was meant to catch local generation traces; MBE was meant to restore them before scoring. We built all three, then took them off one at a time. Official degradations at train time stay — that is the robustness recipe. MBE, RGPA, and the gate do not.
